FAQ

Questions? We've got answers.

Can't find what you need? Contact support from any page, or email support@feelyfeely.com.

Is FeelyFeely free?

Yes. Joining and posting are free with no hidden charges. You only pay if you choose to boost a listing, buy a promotion or upgrade a store plan.

What can I list?

Five types: an item for sale, rent or free; a phone or tablet; a vehicle or motorbike; a home, land or property; or a place, business or service.

How do I sign up?

With a Ghana phone number (+233) or an email. You can sign in with a one-time code or a password.

How do I get a verified badge?

Verify your identity with a Ghana Card and selfie. For property, upload supporting documents during posting — or later.

How do buyers contact me?

By in-app chat, call or inquiry — you choose which options appear on each listing.

Which payments are supported?

Mobile money, Visa/Mastercard, bank transfer, wallet, and pay on delivery or pickup.

My business is already on FeelyFeely. How do I take it over?

Open the place page and choose "Claim a place". Submit proof; once approved, you manage it.

Can my team share one account?

Create an organization or store and invite people with roles — Owner, Admin, Manager or Viewer.

How do giveaways work?

Post with the "For Free" intent. Claimants join the line and accept your pickup policy.

Can I shop from abroad?

Yes. FeelyFeely connects verified sellers in Ghana with shoppers at home and in the diaspora — the UK, US and beyond.

How do I control my privacy?

Use the Privacy Centre in your account to choose what your profile shows, or to deactivate or delete your account.

Where can I use FeelyFeely?

On Android (Google Play), on iPhone by adding it to your Home Screen, and in any browser at feelyfeely.com.

Can I edit a listing after posting?

Yes. Account → Listings shows every listing with its status, price and stats — open one to edit or update it.

Is there a separate site for homes?

Yes — FeelyFeely Homes lives at feelyfeely.homes, focused purely on property.

Prefer to watch?

Short how-to videos walk through your account and posting items, phones, vehicles, homes and places. Follow @feelyfeelygh.
