PART TWO

Getting started

Nine simple steps — from creating your account to managing your first listing.

feelyfeely.com
Step 1 of 9

Create your account

  • Get FeelyFeely from Google Play, or open feelyfeely.com on any device.
  • Tap Login or Signup → Create account, using your phone number (+233) or email.
  • Enter the one-time code we send you — or set a password if you prefer.
GET IT ONGoogle Play ON THE WEBfeelyfeely.com
Step 2 of 9

Find your way around

  • The home page is your start: Explore, Market, Homes and Locals sit along the top.
  • Category circles jump straight to phones, vehicles, homes, fashion and more.
  • Post is always one tap away, top right.
Step 3 of 9

Search and filter

  • Type into the search bar — "a product, a home, a place or anything."
  • Narrow by category, price and location, or switch to the map view.
  • Save the search to get an alert when something new matches.
Step 4 of 9

Save and collect

  • Tap the heart on any listing to save it to your account.
  • Group favourites into Collections — "Apartments", "Sport cars" — and share them.
  • Find everything under Account → Saved and Collections.
Step 5 of 9

Chat, call or inquire

  • Every listing shows Call, Chat and Inquire — plus Add to cart where the seller takes orders.
  • The listing stays attached to the chat, so you never lose track.
  • Never pay a deposit to "hold" an item — use Report if anything feels off.
Step 6 of 9

Tap Post, pick a type

  • Item for sale, rent or free — furniture, electronics, clothes.
  • Mobile phone or tablet · Vehicle or motorbike.
  • Home, land or property · Place, business or service.
feelyfeely.com/listing/post
Step 7 of 9

Add the details and photos

  • Search the catalogue — pick a match and brand, model and specs fill in.
  • Add condition and colour, then generate the description in one tap.
  • Upload up to 10 photos and a video; drag to reorder.
Step 8 of 9

Set your price and publish

  • Enter a price, and choose to allow negotiation or accept offers.
  • Pick your delivery options and how buyers can contact you.
  • Post for free — or boost for more visibility — then tap Publish.
Step 9 of 9

Keep your profile current

  • Open Account → Profile and follow the completion score.
  • Verify your email, phone and ID — a Ghana Card and selfie unlocks a verified badge.
  • Manage listings, messages and notifications from the same menu.
Payments

MoMo, card or cash — all in one place

Buyers pay the way that suits them, and sellers choose what they accept. Card and mobile money payments are processed securely by Paystack.

MoMo

MTN MoMo, Telecel Cash and more, approved on the buyer's phone.

Cards

Visa and Mastercard, online.

Bank

Bank transfer, plus a FeelyFeely wallet for faster checkout.

On delivery

Pay on delivery or on pickup, where the seller allows it.
